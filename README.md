🚀 RemoteFlow

RemoteFlow is a simple and intuitive system that helps companies track where their employees are working and manage leave requests — all in one place.

Whether your team works remotely, on-site, or in a hybrid setup, RemoteFlow keeps everyone aligned without endless emails or confusion.

👩‍💻 For Employees

Mark your daily work status as On-site or Remote

Submit leave requests directly from your dashboard

Track the status of your leave requests in real time

🛠️ For Admins

View your team’s work statuses for any day

Approve or reject leave requests with a single click

Manage everything from one centralized dashboard

📊 Dashboards

RemoteFlow provides two dedicated dashboards:

Employee Dashboard – simple and focused on daily updates

Admin Dashboard – full visibility and control over the team

This separation keeps the experience clear and easy for everyone.</p>
